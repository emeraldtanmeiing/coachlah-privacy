CoachLah Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CoachLah is a scheduling and lesson-counting app for personal trainers. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: CoachLah does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The app stores the information you type into it: gym names, student names and notes, packages, weekly times and lessons. This information is saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, so that the app and its home screen widget can both read it. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, because the app has no server and no account.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, receive or have access to your data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell or share your information.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your iPhone (for example, before a student's last lesson, when a package needs renewing, or when lessons have not been marked). These are created on your device and do not pass through any server. You can turn them off at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

Exporting

When you export lessons, the app creates a spreadsheet file and opens the iPhone's share sheet. Where the file goes (for example Messages, Mail or Files) is up to you and is governed by the app you choose.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup for your iPhone, the app's information may be included in that backup. This is handled by Apple under your own account, and we cannot see it.

Deleting your information

Deleting the CoachLah app from your iPhone removes all the information it stored.

Children

CoachLah is meant for trainers and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version of the app adds a feature that sends data off your phone, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
